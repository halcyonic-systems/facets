Scope: `web/src/SlPane.tsx`, `SlChain.tsx`, `sl/SlEditor.tsx` + `sl/*.ts`, `CoAuthorMode.tsx`, `ReasonerGate.tsx`, `StartSurface.tsx`, `SystemTypeEditor.tsx`, `KernelErrorBoundary.tsx`, `ui.tsx` (Card, Pill, DescriptionField), `index.css` lines 469 to 574 (the editor block), the home door at `HomeScreen.tsx:569-700` and the sandbox header at `sandbox/SandboxSurface.tsx:85-195`. Measurements are computed styles and bounding boxes read from the live page; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from the resolved tokens.

Method note: the shared Chrome window was being resized and its tabs closed by the other auditors throughout, so the widths were produced inside a same-origin iframe of exact CSS size (1440×900, 1024×768, 390×844) hosted in my own tab, which gives the app a true viewport (the 900px `matchMedia` breakpoint fires at 390). The parent window was 1200×726 for most shots, so the 1440 captures are cropped at the right (the margin pane's last 240px) and at the bottom (the drafter's "Turn on" row). The crops lose nothing the findings rest on; the pane itself is the left 1060px.

## 1. Verdict

The SL pane breaks its own first rule twice: the text is not in JetBrains Mono (CodeMirror's base theme wins over `.sl-editor .cm-editor`, so it is Menlo at 13px/18.2px with 22px gutter marks drifting off the lines), and the editor is the one element allowed to shrink, so with three faults and the drafter docked it is 38px tall at 1440 with details open, 36px at 1024 and 0px at 390, where the faults are visible and the line they point at is not.
Everything else is secondary to those two and to the missing focus rings on every text field; the fault list itself reads well in words and badly in form (a red mono paragraph with no link to its line), the controls are pills where the charter says 4px, and five text pairs fall under 4.5:1 in light.

## 3. Faults: how a compile fault should read and sit

What exists: a `--verdict-error` block under the editor, `line N: message — fix: …` in 12px mono, a 2px inset bar and 7% tint on the line, a `!` in the gutter, "N faults" in the status line, "N SL faults" in the status bar. The words are already the best part: each message names the line, the thing, and the fix, which GOV.UK asks for and most editors do not give.

What it should be, in numbers:

- Position. Keep the summary under the editor (the author's eye is on the text, and GOV.UK puts the summary where the eye lands first) but cap it at three rows and give the editor a floor: `min-height: 8 lines` (8 × 22 = 176px) on `.sl-editor`, with the fault list and the drafter yielding first. At 390 the drafter folds to its strip (it already can: `drafterFolded`) whenever faults are present.
- Carrier. One row per fault, 13px Inter (not mono) for the message, the line number as a 13/600 tabular numeral in JetBrains Mono inside a 4px chip, and a 2px `--verdict-error` bar on the row's left edge (GOV.UK's form-group uses 5px; at this density 2px matches the gutter bar so the two read as the same mark). The word "Fault" or "Error" precedes the number for screen readers, visually hidden as GOV.UK does; the block gets `role="alert"` on a new compile.
- Link to the line. Each row is a button: click or Enter sets `focusLine` (the plumbing exists: `selection.focusLine` and `sl-line-flash`, `SlEditor.tsx:130-143`) and moves the caret there. Alt+Up/Down between faults as VS Code's F8 does. The first row receives focus after a failed compile from the button, not from Mod-Enter (the hands are in the text).
- In the editor. The gutter mark becomes a drawn 16px glyph from the icon set (a circle with a bar, 2px stroke) in `--verdict-error`; the line keeps the 2px inset bar and the 7% tint; the message itself appears as an inline zone under the line only when the caret is on it (Zed's inline diagnostics pad 4 columns; VS Code's zone opens on ⌥F8), so the editor never widens.
- Colour with a word. The status line reads "3 faults" in `--verdict-error` and drops "Operational, clean" until the next clean compile, replacing it with "last clean compile: 13 things, 12 relations" in `--text-muted`. The status bar pill cannot show "✓ clean" while faults are present; it shows the faults with the same word.
- Stale. When the author edits past the faults, keep full ink and change the words ("3 faults, text changed since: ⌘⏎ to re-check" becomes "3 faults from the last compile · Re-check" with Re-check as a 4px button), never fade below 4.5:1.
- Dark. The same marks resolve through `--verdict-error` (6.0 on the pane, 6.5 on the editor) and need no retune.
