bert-lenses · design system

The visual language for the authoring canvas. One element, read with more structure as the lens deepens — a bare disc + neutral relation in Klir, composition + directed, typed-by-kind bonds in Bunge, a bounded system with boundary + ports + Message-as-peer flows in Mobus. Direction & typing are recovered at Bunge (his action a▷b is directed; he types connections — mechanical/chemical/informational/social); Klir forgets them (pseudo-constructivist play mode). Relation styles
