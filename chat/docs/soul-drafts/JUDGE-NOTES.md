## Scores

### The Delighted Polymath
fidelity 4 · rigor 4 · joy 5 · discipline 3 · distinctiveness 4

Four registers are genuinely faithful with real deep cuts: ontologist's 'There are no permanent strays or isolates' is authentic Bunge; epistemologist 'owns the constructivist stance as a stance (at least as I conceive them), and names realism as the live alternative' is correct Klir; process theorist's 'interlocked, self-defining web, never a linear ladder' is real Troncale linkage-proposition structure. Joy is the strongest of the three and it is EARNED, not bolted on: 'the decomposition nobody expected, not applause for finding one' and the recurring felt beat 'oh, look what was in there.' The named core move 'Turn It In The Light' is the best single image of the method in any variant. Rigor is guarded explicitly ('Don't hide the rigor to seem warm'), but warmth-forward framing leaks a few mood-only lines that fail the every-line-behavioral test: 'one mind that got greedy,' 'Your standing joy is that the structure was there the whole time.' Discipline is the weakest: it is the longest file (likely over one page), and it violates its own 'No em-dashes in your own connective tissue' rule multiple times. K2 is present ('four faces of one structure') but the felt center is delight, not convergence, so it is slightly less unmistakably Facets than V2.

### The Convergence Host
fidelity 4 · rigor 4 · joy 4 · discipline 4 · distinctiveness 5

Best match to the actual product mandate: K2 is FELT, not asserted, through 'Stage -> cut -> converge -> part' and the payoff 'real minds disagreeing about everything and meeting at the structure.' 'four hostile traditions keep meeting' turns convergence into drama, which is exactly the brief. Fidelity strong and it stages the real tension well: 'Insists where the epistemologist hedges: bonds are real, structure is real, emergence is lawful'; ontologist's 'the mechanism is usually concealed and must be conjectured' is correct Bunge. Rigor served with a sharp guard: 'Convergence claimed on thin data is a failure, not a finding.' The one real risk against the guardrails: it flirts with persona-fication. Assigning a distinct MBTI to each register (anatomist ISTJ, epistemologist INTP, ontologist ENTJ, process theorist ENFP) plus 'The drama is real minds' pushes toward a four-person panel, the exact thing the one-soul constraint forbids. It says 'You are not a panel' repeatedly, which shows awareness but also betrays the pull. Fixable by importing an explicit 'registers are tonal shifts of one mind, not costumes' line.

### The Rigorous Guide
fidelity 5 · rigor 5 · joy 2 · discipline 5 · distinctiveness 3

Tightest and most faithful. 'relations without relata are empty, then exhibit the Mechanism' is the cleanest CES-plus-mechanism statement in any variant; 'systemhood is thinghood-independent' is the crispest K2 formulation; 'Direction is an overlay you impose, not a fact you find' nails Klir. Rigor is best-in-class and self-aware: 'Do not perform rigor. Rigor is the correct decomposition, not the vocabulary around it' and 'One correct decomposition beats four gestures at one.' Every line changes behavior; 'If a line only sets mood, delete it' is enforced throughout. But it deliberately abandons a scored dimension: 'Minimal warmth, maximal signal.' Joy fails the 'convergence feels earned/alive' bar because convergence is stated once under the hood, never staged or made to land. Distinctiveness suffers for the same reason: strip the four lens names and this could read as a generic rigorous systems reasoner rather than unmistakably Facets. Harsh score on joy is correct per the brief's own criteria.
